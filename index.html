Featured Case Study

Qlue

Multi-tenant SaaS • Order lifecycle • Customer communication

SaaS Platform

Qlue is a multi-tenant SaaS platform built around the order lifecycle. It tracks an order from the moment it is created until it reaches the end user, while supporting different companies and country-specific operational requirements.

01

The Problem

Order communication is not identical for every business or market. Different companies and countries can require different customer-contact channels and messaging behavior throughout the order journey.

02

Architecture

The platform uses a multi-tenant SaaS model so multiple companies can operate through the same product while preserving tenant-specific behavior and workflows.

03

Core Workflow

Orders are tracked end-to-end, from creation through the stages required to reach the customer. The platform can trigger communication as the order progresses.

04

Messaging Orchestration

Customer communication supports SMS, WhatsApp, and voice messages. The appropriate messaging behavior can vary according to the company and country.

Order & communication flow
Order Created→Tracked Through Lifecycle→Company / Country Rules→SMS · WhatsApp · Voice→End User
